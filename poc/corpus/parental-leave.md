# Parental Leave

## Entitlement

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers
receive 8 weeks, fully paid. Both apply equally to birth, adoption, and surrogacy, and
there is no minimum length of service required.

## Taking the leave

Leave may be taken in up to three separate blocks within the first 18 months. Blocks must
be at least two weeks long.

## Notice

Give at least 60 days' notice where circumstances allow. We understand this is not always
possible and no one has ever been penalised for short notice.

## Returning

A phased return is available: 60% hours at full pay for the first four weeks back.

Your role is held for the duration of your leave. Performance review cycles and salary
review are not affected by leave, and time on leave counts as service for all purposes.

## Pregnancy loss and fertility treatment

Up to 10 days of paid leave is available for pregnancy loss, for either parent, and for
fertility treatment appointments. This is not deducted from PTO and does not require
detail to be shared with your manager.
