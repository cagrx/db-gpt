# Business Travel

## Booking

Book all travel through the company travel system. Bookings made outside the system are
reimbursed only in genuine emergencies, and you should expect questions.

Book at least 14 days in advance where possible — fares within 14 days average roughly
60% higher.

## Flights

Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights over 6
hours. Business class requires director approval and is normally reserved for flights over
10 hours arriving the same day as a working commitment.

## Ground transport

Use public transport or standard ride-hailing. Car hire requires a reason recorded on the
claim.

## Per diem

Where meals are not otherwise covered, the per diem is $75 per day domestic, $95
international. Per diem does not require receipts.

## Approval

Domestic travel is approved by your manager. International travel requires director
approval. Travel to a location under a government advisory requires People Operations
sign-off regardless of cost.
