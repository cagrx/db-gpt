# Paid Time Off

Harbour Systems provides 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year for all
full-time employees. PTO accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month.

## Carryover

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year.
Days beyond that limit are forfeited on 31 December. Carried-over days must be used by
31 March.

## Requesting time off

Submit requests through the People portal at least 10 business days in advance for
absences of three days or more. Shorter absences require 2 business days' notice. Your
manager approves or declines within 3 business days.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO and is not capped. Absences of more than 5 consecutive
working days require a note from a medical practitioner.

## Public holidays

Harbour Systems observes 10 public holidays per year, published each December for the
following year. Public holidays do not count against PTO.
