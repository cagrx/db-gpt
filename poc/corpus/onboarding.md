# Your First Two Weeks

## Before day one

IT ships your laptop to arrive the working day before you start. If it hasn't arrived,
contact it-support@ — do not wait until your first morning.

## Day one

- Collect your hardware security key
- Complete account setup and MFA enrolment
- Meet your onboarding buddy, assigned before you start
- Team lunch, on the company

## Week one

Complete the four required modules: Security Essentials, Data Handling, Code of Conduct,
and Accessibility Basics. Each takes about 45 minutes.

Your manager will schedule a 30-day goal-setting conversation during your first week.

## Week two

Ship something small. Every team keeps a list of small, low-risk starter tasks for exactly
this purpose. The point is not the task — it is getting through the full cycle of writing,
reviewing, and releasing once, while there is plenty of help available.

## The buddy system

Your onboarding buddy is a peer, not your manager, and is explicitly there for the
questions you would feel awkward asking anyone else. The arrangement formally lasts 90
days.
