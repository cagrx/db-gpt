# Remote and Hybrid Work

Harbour Systems operates a hybrid model. Employees assigned to an office location are
expected on site 2 days per week. Teams choose which days and publish them in the team
calendar.

## Fully remote roles

Some roles are designated fully remote at the point of hire. This is recorded in your
offer letter. Fully remote employees are expected on site for the twice-yearly team
gatherings, with travel covered by the company.

## Home office stipend

A one-time stipend of $500 is available for home office equipment, claimable in your
first 90 days. An annual $150 top-up is available thereafter for consumables and
replacements.

## Working from another country

Working outside your country of employment for more than 20 working days per year
requires approval from People Operations, because it creates tax obligations for both you
and the company. Requests should be made at least 30 days in advance.

## Core hours

Teams maintain core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 in their primary timezone, during which
meetings may be scheduled. Outside core hours, scheduling is by agreement.
