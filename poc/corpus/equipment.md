# Equipment

## Laptops

Every employee receives a laptop appropriate to their role. Engineering roles default to
the higher-specification configuration. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or sooner if
the machine is materially affecting your work — tell your manager, this is a low bar and
we would rather replace a machine than lose a week of someone's time to it.

## Accessories

A monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset are provided on request for both office and home
use. Ordering is through the IT portal and does not require approval.

## Phones

Phones are provided for on-call engineers and roles requiring frequent travel. Others may
claim a $40 monthly allowance toward personal device use for work.

## Repairs and replacement

Report faults through the IT portal. Loaner machines are available same-day at office
locations and next-day by courier.

## Leaving

Equipment is returned on your last working day, or by prepaid courier for remote
employees. Laptops older than 3 years may be purchased at a nominal fee.
