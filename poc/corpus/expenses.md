# Expense Policy

## Limits

| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Meals (per person, per day) | $75 |
| Hotel (per night, standard cities) | $250 |
| Hotel (per night, high-cost cities) | $375 |
| Client entertainment (per person) | $150 |

High-cost cities are London, New York, San Francisco, Singapore, Tokyo and Zurich.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for any expense over $25. Photograph the receipt and attach it to
the expense claim. Claims without required receipts are returned unpaid.

## Deadlines

Submit expenses within 30 days of the date incurred. Claims older than 60 days require
director approval and are rarely granted.

## Approval

Expenses under $500 are approved by your manager. Between $500 and $5,000 requires
director approval. Above $5,000 requires the finance team.

## Not reimbursable

Personal entertainment, in-flight upgrades not booked through the travel system, traffic
fines, and minibar charges.
