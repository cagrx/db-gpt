# Benefits Summary

## Health cover

Medical, dental, and vision cover begins on your first day of employment. There is no
waiting period. The company pays 100% of the employee premium and 75% for dependants.

## Retirement

Harbour Systems matches retirement contributions at 100% of the first 4% of salary
contributed. Matching contributions vest immediately.

## Learning budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of $2,000 for courses, conferences, books,
and certifications. It does not carry over between years. Manager approval is required
for individual items above $500.

## Wellness

An annual wellness stipend of $600 covers gym membership, fitness equipment, therapy, and
similar. Claim it through the normal expense system under the "Wellness" category.

## Life and disability

Life cover at 4x annual salary and long-term disability cover at 60% of salary are
provided at no cost.
