# Security Essentials

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory on all company accounts. Hardware security
keys are issued to everyone with production access and are strongly recommended for
everyone else. SMS-based verification is not permitted.

## Passwords

Use the company password manager. Do not reuse passwords between company and personal
accounts. Passwords are never to be shared, including with IT support — no member of IT
will ever ask for your password.

## Phishing

Report suspected phishing using the "Report Message" button in your mail client. Do not
forward suspicious mail to colleagues. Reporting a message that turns out to be
legitimate carries no penalty; we would far rather investigate a false alarm.

## Devices

Company laptops have full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes.
Report lost or stolen devices to security@ immediately, at any hour. There is no penalty
for losing a device; there is a serious problem with not reporting it quickly.

## Access reviews

Access to production systems is reviewed quarterly. Access not used in 90 days is revoked
automatically and can be re-requested.
